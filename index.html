DESERT HOMESTEAD

catch the rain · hold the soil · build your home

The desert doesn´t lack water — it loses it. Every storm races off the bare land in an hour. Your job: slow it, spread it, sink it.

⛏ Swales catch the sheet-flow running downhill.  🪵 Beaver-dam analogs pond the wash and green its banks.  🪨 Rock dams silt the little creeks into farmland.  🌱 Crops drink — 2 moisture a day — so put water where the roots are.  🧱 Earthbags raise your home from the dirt your swales pay you.

Field school first — eight tiny one-click lessons, one per tool. Then ten levels climbing from saguaro country to the pinyon-juniper rim. Deterministic storms — the forecast is a promise. Finish at par pace for ⭐⭐⭐, and mind the rattlers. 🐍

Drag orbits · scroll zooms · click works the land · shift-drag paints · space ends the day · ⏱ turns on the countdown clock if you want the pressure.
