A permaculture castle defense. The monsoon comes down your watershed as living waves — water monsters, rockslides, heat imps, raiders — and everything you build fights back. Swales drink the flood into money, your plants are the towers, and thirst is their ammo.

⛏ Swale pairs slurp water monsters and bank the 💧.  🌽 Plant towers sling, lob, and smash — while their beds stay wet.  🪵 Dams wall the wash and snag log rams.  ☀️ Dry waves come to burn what you banked.  🦫 Keep a dam wet and the beavers finish the job.

Five sites, five seasons — from a gentle bajada to the whole watershed. Hold the homestead through every wave. 🦫

Drag orbits · scroll zooms · tap a card then the map to build · tap monsters to smack them · ⏩ doubles time · ⛈ calls the storm early.
